A quiet intelligence over your memories

Preview only. Demo numbers and sample photographs. No Photos access, nothing is compressed or deleted, and the Swift app is unchanged. Savings are estimates until copies are checked.

The idea

  • The faded photograph is a memory being revisited, almost a replay.
  • The glass is Everkeep working quietly over it.
  • The dial is the only navigation. Drag or flick it, tap a category, or use the arrow keys.
